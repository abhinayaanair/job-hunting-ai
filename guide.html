How this session works

PART 1 · ~90 MIN

The specialists

The best tool for each job: resume, interview, contacts, applying. Each one is excellent in its own lane.

PART 2

The all-rounder: Claude

One subscription that covers every lane: job-hunting plugins, live job-board connectors and an agent in your Chrome browser.

PART 3

Your questions

Your real job-search problems, solved live, plus a 7-day action plan.

The specialist vs the all-rounder. In a company you hire specialists: a resume writer, an interview coach, a contact researcher, a market researcher, an application assistant. Each is the best at one thing, and you pay each one. Or you hire one all-rounder who does a bit of everything and connects it all together. Specialist tools still win their own lane: if all you need is deep contact discovery, Clay is excellent. But if you want contacts, research, a tailored resume, applications and tracking working together, one assistant like Claude, with job-hunting skills, connectors and a browser agent, does the whole workflow.

Claude in Chrome: set it up

  1. You need a paid Claude plan (Pro or higher).
  2. In desktop Google Chrome, open the Chrome Web Store, search for Claude and click Add to Chrome.
  3. Pin the extension, sign in with your Claude account and open the side panel.
  4. Give it a task: copy one of the Claude prompts below.
  5. Keep approvals on and review everything before you submit an application.

Prompt library

Click Copy, paste into the tool, and replace anything in [brackets] with your own details.

Resume & ATS (ChatGPT or Claude)

Prompt 1 · ATS-optimised resume
Act as an expert resume writer. Create a 100% ATS-optimised, reverse-chronological resume using industry-specific keywords from the attached job description.
Tailor the resume exactly to the job description. Include only relevant information, and make sure each section has JD keywords.
Work-experience bullets must show measurable outcomes, numbers and results.
Attach the job description and your current resume.
Prompt 2 · ATS score check
Act as an advanced Applicant Tracking System (ATS) and a professional recruiter. I will share my resume and the job description I am applying for. Give me an ATS compatibility score out of 100.
- Analyse my resume against the job description as an ATS would
- Highlight formatting issues that may reduce ATS readability
- Check role alignment with the job description
- Point out bullet points that are too generic or unclear
- Suggest exact keyword-rich rewrites for improvement
Run this after prompt 1 to check the new version.

STAR interview answers (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini)

Prompt 1 · Bullet → STAR answer
Take this resume bullet and expand it into a strong interview answer using the STAR method. Make it sound confident, structured and natural for a 60-second response.

Bullet: [Built a RAG chatbot that reduced support tickets by 30%.]
Swap in any bullet from your own resume.
Prompt 2 · Rough notes → STAR answer
Help me answer "Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership." Here are my rough notes: [AI chatbot launch was slipping, too many feature requests, I prioritised the 3 that mattered, ran daily check-ins, we shipped on time]. Convert this into the STAR framework for a confident 60-second response.
Messy notes are fine. That is the point.

Comet (agentic browser)

Prompt 1 · Finding jobs
I am looking for a high-paying [AI developer / AI consultant] job in [Bengaluru]. With the help of the resume that I have attached here, tell me the first 3 jobs that you can find with Easy Apply that match my profile.
Open LinkedIn in Comet first, attach your resume, then ask the Assistant.
Prompt 2 · Applying (Easy Apply + normal)
I want to apply for the job of [AI Consultant] at [X Company]. Use my attached resume to fill in the application, and stop before submitting so I can review it.
You approve every final submission. Check personal details, salary and declarations.
Prompt 3 · Personalised connection note
Look at [name]'s profile and create a connection note for them. It should be personalised.

OR: I want to send a personalised connection note to the hiring manager of the [Y role] opening at [Z Company]. Help me write it.
Open the person's LinkedIn profile first so the agent can read it.
Prompt 4 · Tailor resume in Google Docs
I want to apply for this job, [X role], but I don't think my resume is currently a good fit. Review the job requirements and modify my resume in Google Docs to be a better fit for this job.
Keep the original. Ask the agent to work on a copy.
Prompt 5 · Summarise articles & videos
Summarise this video.

Move to the next interesting part of the video.
Great for company podcasts, CEO interviews and earnings calls before an interview.
Prompt 6 · Payments (show only)
Buy [product] from [Amazon] and pay.
Demo the idea only. Never let an agent pay or submit without your explicit approval.

Claude & Claude in Chrome

Prompt 1 · Find & shortlist
Search LinkedIn Jobs for [AI Developer / AI Consultant] roles in [Bengaluru] posted in the last 7 days. Compare each one against my attached resume and give me a shortlist of the top 5 with a fit score out of 100 and one line on why.
Use Claude in Chrome with LinkedIn open, or the Indeed / ZipRecruiter connector.
Prompt 2 · Tailor & apply
Open this job: [paste link]. Tailor my resume to its requirements as a new Google Doc, draft a 120-word cover note, then fill in the application form. Stop before the final submit so I can review everything.
Always review before you submit.
Prompt 3 · Recruiter outreach
Find the recruiter or hiring manager for the [role] opening at [company] on LinkedIn and draft a personalised connection note under 300 characters that mentions one specific thing from their profile.
You send the note yourself. Never let it mass-message.
Prompt 4 · Application tracker
Create an Excel tracker with columns Company, Role, Link, Date applied, Contact, Follow-up date and Status, and add every job we shortlisted today. Set the follow-up date to 5 days after applying.
Uses the built-in xlsx skill: you get a real Excel file.
Prompt 5 · Job Spotter plugin
Using Job Spotter, find [AI Engineer] roles in [Bengaluru], match them against my CV, and prepare applications for the top 3, including a tailored resume and cover letter for each.
Install Job Spotter from the plugin directory first.

Tool wants a work email?

Make a free domain email

Use Cloudflare Email Routing or Zoho Mail Free to create an address like hello@yourname.in. Most B2B tools accept it as a work email.

Use finders that accept Gmail

Apollo.io (generous free plan), Prospeo, Hunter.io, Snov.io and Lusha (limited free credits).

Don't borrow your employer's

Never use a corporate email for personal job hunting or lead generation unless your employer explicitly allows it.
