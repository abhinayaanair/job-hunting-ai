The STAR framework

S

Situation

Briefly describe the context.

"During a key project, our team started missing deadlines."
T

Task

Explain your responsibility.

"I was responsible for timely delivery and team coordination."
A

Action

Highlight what YOU did.

"I identified bottlenecks, reassigned tasks and introduced daily check-ins."
R

Result

Share a measurable outcome.

"We met the deadline, and I was promoted for my performance."
Example: "I handled a team delay in a project effectively and got promoted for it." Press → to build it with STAR.
Your turn · practise STAR
INTERVIEW QUESTION

"Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership."

S

Situation

Think: Set the scene. Where were you, and what was going wrong? Keep it to 1–2 lines.

"In my final-year project, our 5-member team lost momentum two weeks before the deadline."

T

Task

Think: What was your responsibility? What was at stake if nobody stepped up?

"I took ownership of getting the project back on track and submitting on time."

A

Action

Think: What did YOU do? Specific steps, decisions, how you guided people. Say "I", not "we".

"I split work by strengths, ran 15-minute daily stand-ups, and personally unblocked the hardest module."

R

Result

Think: What changed? Give a number, feedback or recognition, and what you learned.

"We submitted 2 days early, got the top grade in our batch, and I was asked to mentor juniors."

60–90 seconds "I", not "we" End with a number Real story, not a textbook answer
